Before you ask

Questions I get every week

Do I own the site when it's finished?

Yes, all of it — the domain, the files, the accounts. It's written into the agreement. If you ever want to move to another designer, you send them a folder and they can pick it up.

Do I have to write the words myself?

No. Most owners hand me a pile of notes and old brochures, we talk for an hour, and I write the first draft. You edit it. You know your trade better than I do, so your voice stays in it.

What if I already have a site I hate?

That's the Rebuild package ($1,200). I keep what's earning you traffic — page addresses, reviews, listings — and replace everything underneath. Your existing site stays up until the new one is ready to swap in.

Will I show up on Google?

The site will be built the way search engines like: fast, readable, properly labeled, with your business details consistent everywhere. That's the foundation. Ranking above an established competitor also takes reviews and time, and I'll tell you honestly where you stand.

Can I edit it myself later?

Yes. Text, photos, hours, and prices are set up for you to change without touching code, and I'll record a short video walkthrough for your own site so you're not searching through generic help articles. Or skip it entirely with Care & Feeding ($65/month) — I make the edits for you.

How do payments work?

For the Starter Nest, Rebuild, or Full Build: half to reserve the build slot, half on launch day. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices — if the scope changes, we agree on the new number first. Care & Feeding is billed monthly, cancel anytime.
