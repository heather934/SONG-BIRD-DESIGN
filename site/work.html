Under Pressure Custom Apparel

underpressurecustomapparel.com

The whole business runs on customers bringing their own artwork, so the store had to make that the easy part. A shopper picks a garment, uploads their logo straight from their phone, sets sizes and quantities, and pays — and the shop owner gets a fully itemized order in her inbox the moment the payment clears.

Products, prices, and shipping options are edited from an admin panel, so the store changes without a developer. It runs on Cloudflare rather than a hosted store platform, which means no monthly percentage taken off the top.

  • Artwork upload from any phone, resized and served automatically
  • Cart is saved before checkout, so a stalled payment doesn't lose the order
  • Payments verified against PayPal before an order is ever recorded
  • Duplicate payment notices are caught and ignored
